## 6. Confidence statement

**Geometry/structure of the chawl: ~70% covered by OSS.** Kenney RPG Urban Kit + Surt Town Tiles + George Bailey interiors + Kenney UI is enough to lay out an alley, a courtyard, a room, and the whole UI shell without writing a single new tile.

**Cultural specificity: ~25% covered by OSS.** The chai stall, dabbawala bicycle, AC compressor, hanging laundry, Indian-formal-wear NPC overlays, religious-icon corner, and the ₹ glyph all need in-house pixel work. This is unavoidable — the open-source pixel-art commons skews heavily Western-medieval / Japanese / generic-modern, with virtually nothing chawl-flavoured under acceptable licences. Plan accordingly: **the chawl will look like a chawl only after ~60-80 hours of in-house re-skin + hand-pixel work on top of the OSS substrate.**

**Cannot ship the district from OSS alone.** Can ship a *playable greybox* in 1-2 days. Can ship a *visually distinct chawl* in ~2 artist-weeks after this base is in place.

## B.7 — Confidence statement (revised after Round 2)

**Geometry/structure of the chawl: ~85% covered by OSS (up from ~70% in Round 1).** Ninja Adventure + Sharm Adobe + GrafxKid City Mega together provide enough urban geometry, NPC variety, and UI primitives that the chawl can be laid out, populated, and dialog-enabled without writing a tile from scratch.

**Cultural specificity: ~30% covered by OSS (up from ~25%).** The Round-2 hunt added city-citizens (GrafxKid) and industrial details (0x72) but the *Indian-specific* gap (chai stall, dabbawala bicycle, saree/kurta overlays, iron grilles, hanging laundry, religious-icon corner, Devanagari pixel font) is essentially unchanged. **This is a structural property of the open-source pixel-art commons in May 2026 — not a research failure on our side.** Indian-flavoured pixel art lives almost entirely on commission-only artist Patreon tiers or in licence-locked itch.io packs.

**Ship-grade visual without commission:** **NO.** With Round 2 we can ship a *playable, visually consistent, pixel-clean* chawl that passes a "no placeholder" smell-test on first glance. We *cannot* ship a *culturally legible* chawl without ~60h of in-house re-skin + saree/kurta overlay work + the chai-stall and dabbawala-bike commissions. The flex test ("could we put a screenshot of this on the website?") passes for a generic-pixel chawl at Round 2, but the answer to "does this look like a *Mumbai* chawl?" still requires the commission spend.

**Confidence levels:**
- Visual *consistency* (one art style, no Frankenstein mix): **high** — Round 2 narrowed PRIMARY picks to a small set of authors with compatible palettes.
- Visual *quality* per-tile: **high** — Ninja Adventure, Sharm, GrafxKid, Pixel Frog are all production-grade.
- Visual *cultural authenticity*: **medium-low without commission**; **high after the ₹156k / 195h spend.**
- Licence cleanliness: **very high** — 74% CC0, 14% CC-BY 4.0, attribution easily fits a single in-game credits screen.
