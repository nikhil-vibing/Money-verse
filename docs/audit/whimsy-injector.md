# Whimsy Injector — Money-verse Audit

*Pillar #8 binding: no dopamine bursts. Whimsy = warmth + cultural specificity.*

## 1. What's Missing — 5 Flat Moments

1. **Greeting is a wall.** Press E on Ravi → line drops. *Add: 0.3s `☕` puff + kettle ting on bubble open.*
2. **Biscuit is a sprite, not a dog.** *Add: trails the player 3s after a pet, then sits and watches you walk off. No reward.*
3. **The chawl is silent.** No train, no temple bell at 6pm, no aunty arguing two floors up. *Add: ambient layer keyed to in-game clock.*
4. **"Press E to talk" is sterile.** Maya's: *"Press E — Maya is pouring tea."* Postman's: *"Press E — Postman is tapping his watch."*
5. **Quest envelopes are static rectangles.** *Add: paper-crinkle sfx + 1-frame flick. Untouched 20s → Biscuit sniffs it.*

## 2. Indian-Flavoured Easter Eggs (12)

1. **Monsoon puddle** reflects a rotating Bollywood poster (*Sholay*, *DDLJ*, *3 Idiots*) — visible only 18:00–20:00 after rain.
2. **Bhola's knock** is always seven rapid raps. Players will count.
3. **Maa interrupts**: ignore any dialog 5 min → landline rings — *"Beta, busy ho kya? Khaana?"*
4. **Bandar snatch** — leave an envelope on a ledge >30s, a monkey swings through, drops it two tiles away. Recoverable.
5. **Train horn** every ~90s; at 19:42 it's the *Rajdhani* — longer horn.
6. **Sushila's chalkboard**: *"Pyaaz ₹50. Tamatar ₹40. Mood: kharaab."* Third line changes daily.
7. **Ravi's transistor** plays muffled cricket; Sundays, *Vividh Bharati* film songs.
8. **Dipu kaka's chair creaks** the first time you sit beside him. Always the same creak.
9. **Lakshmi's dabba stack** clinks differently when she runs (late) vs walks. Audio tell, no UI.
10. **Holi week** — three pixels of pink powder cling to the player sprite for one in-game day.
11. **Maa's achaar jar** appears in the room two days after the phone mention. If you don't tap it, Biscuit does.
12. **Cricket-ball thud** — a tennis ball rolls in from off-screen. Kick it back → unseen "wah!" from the kid.

## 3. Micro-Interactions (5)

1. **Confirm button** bounces 1.05× with a soft *thock* on **conversational** picks. Never on financial commits.
2. **Coin spin on first grant** — Maya hands ₹500, one coin spins 0.8s, clinks. Once per run. No confetti.
3. **Saffron pulse** around the *envelope* (not the player, not a score) on a correct allocation. 600ms. Replaces "Correct!" popup.
4. **NPC double-take** — wear something new → nearest NPC turns head, `?` floats 0.4s. No auto-dialog.
5. **Bead-curtain sway** — walking through the doorway clacks the beads 0.6s.

## 5. Single Biggest Whimsy Move

**Maa never appears on screen — her achaar jar does.** Tuesday's call mentions pickle via Lakshmi. Thursday, a glass jar with hand-written marker — *"Mira ke liye — kam mirchi"* — sits on the player's table. No XP, no quest. Stays the rest of the run. *A mother's promise the game remembered.*

## Top 3 + The Share

**Top 3**: (1) the **monsoon puddle reflecting a rotating Bollywood poster at dusk**, (2) **Biscuit trailing the player 3s after a pet** before sitting back down, (3) **Maa interrupting any 5-min dialog stall with "Beta, khaana khaaya?"** — three moments that turn the chawl from a level into a place that notices you. **Share-move**: Maa's hand-labelled achaar jar appearing on the table two days after the phone call — no popup, no quest, no reward — the game quietly keeping her word.
