## 1. The brand frame

**One-sentence frame.** *Ninja Money-verse is a ninja school for your money — a 2D pixel-art RPG where you train financial discipline as if it were a martial art: one stance at a time, one breath at a time, with a sensei who never lectures.*

**Paragraph.** You wake in an old quarter of a city you've never seen. A green-clad ninja — that is you — is given a single envelope, a single rule, and a single neighbour who has done this longer than you have. Each district is a chamber. Each chamber teaches one virtue. Income minus expenses is a stance. Compounding is a still pool. Risk and return are caution and strike. By the time you leave, you do not "know about money" — your body does. That is the difference between an article and a dojo.

**Primary tagline.** *Train your money like a ninja trains the body.*

**Alternative taglines (ranked).**
1. *A ninja school for your money.*
2. *Master the stance before the strike.*
3. *Quiet discipline. Loud results.*
4. *One coin. One breath. One lesson at a time.*
5. *Wax on, save on.* (Miyagi nod — campaign tag, not the homepage)

**Why this works.** The protagonist is already a ninja (Ninja Adventure CC0 asset pack). The metaphor describes the *teaching method* we already have — mastery gates, daily ritual, forgiveness, hide-the-lesson-in-the-verb — using a frame that translates without footnotes in every market. It also gives us a vocabulary upgrade for what currently feels awkward in English: SIP, FD, NPS, "envelope budgeting" become *forms*, *vaults*, and *paths*.

## 2. Seven masters of teaching discipline through metaphor

**M1 — Mr. Miyagi / *The Karate Kid* (1984).** Pat Morita's Oscar-nominated turn; canonical reference for "the lesson hidden in the chore." Signature: *wax-on / wax-off* — the student performs an unrelated chore until the body owns the muscle memory; *you have been learning all along* is the payoff. **Application:** the chai-receipt quest *is* a wax-on quest. Reframe the chore as a **training form**: not "tracking expenses" but *practising the First Form*.

**M2 — Naruto's chakra-control arcs (Kishimoto).** The tree-climbing arc is the most-cited "training-as-foundation" example in popular shōnen. Signature: the boring foundation gates the exciting technique — no rasengan until you balance on a tree. **Application:** mirrors Khan-mastery. Budgeting (chakra control) gates investing (rasengan).

**M3 — Avatar: The Last Airbender (DiMartino / Konietzko).** Animation-curriculum reference for elemental-discipline pedagogy. Signature: each element teaches a different cognitive virtue — earth = patience, water = adaptability, fire = focused intensity, air = freedom. Toph cannot learn earth until she stops fighting it; the *student's psychology* is the gate. **Application:** each of our 7 districts teaches a different financial virtue, not just a different topic. Name the virtue, not the instrument.

**M4 — Demon Slayer's breathing forms (Gotouge).** Best-selling 2019-2020 manga; *Mugen Train* highest-grossing Japanese film at release. Signature: *distinct named techniques*, each one principle deeply practised — *Water Breathing, First Form: Surface Slash.* The naming is mnemonic. **Application:** each concept gets a *form* name — *First Form: The Envelope. Second Form: Pay Yourself First. Third Form: The Jar That Doesn't Open.*

**M5 — *36th Chamber of Shaolin* (Lau Kar-leung, 1978).** Structural reference for *Kill Bill*, *Cobra Kai*, *Avatar*. Signature: *the training-room structure* — 35 chambers, one virtue each (balance, sight, fists, wrist, wood-dummy); the 36th is the world. **Cleanest match to our 7-district structure.** **Application:** rename districts as **chambers**, one named virtue each. The 7th chamber (Retirement) is where the student begins to *teach others*.

**M6 — Ryan Holiday, modern Stoicism.** *The Obstacle Is the Way*, *The Daily Stoic*. Translates Aurelius/Epictetus/Seneca into modern practical discipline. Signature: *short, declarative, present-tense sentences.* Observation, then pause. *The impediment to action advances action.* **Application:** **this is the voice register for sensei dialogue.** Hinglish vocatives drop; the cadence stays.

**M7 — Robert Greene, *Mastery* (2012).** Codified the apprenticeship model from biographies of Da Vinci, Darwin, Coltrane. Signature: *three stages* — deep observation → skills acquisition → experimentation. **The sensei does not push; the sensei waits.** **Application:** Tobias — already "never volunteers a lecture unless asked twice" — is the prototype. Apply to *every* sensei.

**Honourable mentions.** Naval Ravikant — short-form aphorisms unlocked as scroll fragments at chamber graduations. Cobra Kai — Miyagi-Do vs Cobra Kai is the *Wren vs Bhola* axis we already have.

## 3. Consensus + divergences + our choice

**Where 4+ masters agree.**
1. **Hide the lesson in the chore.** (Miyagi, Naruto, ATLA, Demon Slayer, 36 Chambers.) Identical to pillar #1.
2. **Name the form after the body learns it.** Identical to glossary-on-demand.
3. **One virtue per chamber.** Identical to pillar #2.
4. **The sensei waits.** (Miyagi, Holiday, Greene.) New — *no proactive lectures from NPCs.*
5. **Short sentences, present tense.** New voice register.

**Where masters diverge.**

| Question | Camp A | Camp B | Our choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tone | Cobra Kai — adrenalised rivalry | Miyagi / Holiday — contemplative | **B.** Pillar #8 forbids adrenalised. Bhola is a tempter, not a rival. |
| Naming density | Demon Slayer — every technique named | Miyagi — almost nothing named | **Hybrid.** 7 chambers + ~7 forms per chamber. Glossary on demand. |
| Cultural register | ATLA — invented pan-Asian world | Naruto — heavily Japan-coded | **A.** Invent a generic-Asian world. No real-Japan pastiche. |
| Sensei archetype | Greene — silent until asked | Demon Slayer — vocal one-liners | **A for ambient, B for quests.** |

## 6. Currency + financial-instrument reframe

### Currency: **`$` — read in-fiction as "the coin of the realm"**

| Option | Pros | Cons | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`$`** | Universally readable; lowest cognitive load; works on every keyboard | Slight US lean | **8/10** |
| `coins` / `G` (gold) | RPG-native; symbol-free | Loses "real money" anchor that makes lessons transferable | 6/10 |
| `ryo` (Naruto) | Deeply ninja-flavoured | Pastiches Japan; alienates non-fans | 4/10 |

**Choice: `$`.** UI shows `$15,000`; characters say *"fifteen thousand"* without the symbol. This gives keyboard-friendly numerals, immediate transferability to the player's real wallet, no Indian-rupee dependence, and no fake-fantasy currency that breaks the financial-education promise. Numbers map ÷10 from rupees for clean round figures (₹15,000 salary → $1,500; ₹4,800 rent → $480; ₹70/day tea → $7/day; ₹50,000 emergency fund → $5,000). Whole numbers only for v1 — no floats to parse, faithful to envelope budgeting.

## 7. Anti-patterns to avoid

1. **Don't pastiche Japan.** No fake-Japanese place names ("Hyokuro Village"). No bowing animations. No hiragana easter eggs. Use neutral fantasy-Asian (*First Dojo, Still Pool, Path of the Elders*). The art speaks the visual register; the writing should not over-claim.
2. **Don't appropriate.** "Sensei" is fine (OED-listed). "Bushido," "samurai," "katana," religious imagery, traditional clothing references — out. Bow at the mat, not the player.
3. **Don't lose pillar #8.** Ninja training is contemplative, not adrenalised. No "leveling up," no power-ups, no XP bars, no tournament-rivalry arc.
4. **Don't make the sensei lecture.** Greene's rule: *the sensei waits.* If Sensei Wren volunteers a finance fact, the apprenticeship model collapses. NPCs respond when asked; they observe in passing; they wait.
5. **Don't fetishise austerity.** Sethi pillar holds. The point is *conscious* spending, not penny-pinching. The Lender is the antagonist because they exploit shame, not because spending is bad.
6. **Don't lose distinctive NPC voice.** The biggest risk of an international rewrite is flattening every NPC into calm-corporate. Cadence diversity is mandatory: Wren slow-warm; Arlo hype-fast; Lia two-word-staccato; Master Tobias long-pause; The Lender honey-smooth.
7. **Don't change the math.** Every dollar amount maps to the equivalent rupee amount on a clean scale. Lesson integrity preserved.
8. **Don't drop the diverse cast.** *Wren, Arlo, Mara, Lia, Tobias, Kai* stay. The world stays implicitly South-Asian in the art. Only the *language register* internationalises.
