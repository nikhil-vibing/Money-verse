## Master Evaluation Scorecard

Rate each potential master 1-5 on these criteria. Select those scoring 15+ out of 25.

| Criteria | 1 (Low) | 5 (High) |
|---|---|---|
| **Proven Results** | Claims without evidence | Specific, verifiable metrics |
| **Documentation** | Barely wrote about methods | Books, courses, extensive blog |
| **Recency** | Methods from 20+ years ago | Active and current (last 2-3 years) |
| **Transferability** | Hyper-specific to their unique case | Generalizable framework anyone can follow |
| **Relevance** | Tangentially related field | Exact same goal in same/similar field |

## Pattern Extraction Template

Use this when synthesizing across multiple masters:

```
PATTERN: [Name the pattern in 3-5 words]
FREQUENCY: [How many masters exhibit this? e.g., 4/5]
DESCRIPTION: [What is it?]
EVIDENCE:
  - [Master A]: [How they demonstrated it + result]
  - [Master B]: [How they demonstrated it + result]
  - [Master C]: [How they demonstrated it + result]
CONFIDENCE: [HIGH if 4+/5 masters agree, MEDIUM if 3/5, LOW if 2/5]
APPLICATION TO USER: [How this applies to the user's specific context]
```

## Common Master Archetypes by Domain

### Content & Social Media
- The Algorithm Decoder (understands platform mechanics)
- The Storyteller (narrative-first approach)
- The System Builder (repeatable frameworks)
- The Community Architect (engagement-first growth)

### Career & Skills
- The Self-Taught Prodigy (documented their learning path)
- The Career Pivoter (changed fields successfully)
- The Mentor/Teacher (helps others achieve what they achieved)
- The Portfolio Builder (showed work to get opportunities)

### Business & Revenue
- The Bootstrapper (built without funding)
- The Scaler (took small to big)
- The Methodologist (created repeatable business frameworks)
- The Turnaround Artist (fixed broken businesses)

### Product & Design
- The Taste-Maker (known for exceptional craft)
- The User Obsessive (research-driven design)
- The Systematic Designer (design systems, processes)
- The Rapid Shipper (speed + quality balance)

When selecting masters, try to include at least 2 different archetypes for diverse perspectives.

## Deliverable Quality Checklist

Before presenting the final output, verify:

- [ ] Every major recommendation traces to at least one studied master
- [ ] Consensus patterns are clearly distinguished from optional approaches
- [ ] User's specific constraints have been addressed (not generic advice)
- [ ] The "Master's Margin Note" section is included
- [ ] Divergence points are presented as strategic choices, not confusion
- [ ] The output matches the format the user requested
- [ ] Anti-patterns from masters are included (what NOT to do)
- [ ] At least 2 resources are recommended for the user's independent study
